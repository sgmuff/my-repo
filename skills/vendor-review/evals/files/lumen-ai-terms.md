# Lumen Notes AI Features: Supplemental Terms (updated August 2026)

*Test fixture. Fictional.*

1. **What the AI does.** Lumen Notes joins meetings on your calendar, records audio and video, produces a transcript, a summary, action items, and a "sentiment and engagement" score for each participant, and can send follow-up emails to participants on the host's behalf.
2. **Model providers.** Lumen uses third-party foundation models, currently from providers listed at lumen.example/subprocessors, hosted in the United States.
3. **Training.** Lumen does not train its models on Customer Content for Enterprise plan customers. For other plans, Lumen may use de-identified Customer Content to improve its models unless the Customer opts out in admin settings. This section is governed by the Lumen AI Policy, which Lumen may update.
4. **Retention.** Recordings are retained for 12 months by default. Transcripts and summaries are retained until deleted by the Customer. Model providers may retain prompts for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
5. **Outputs.** As between the parties, Customer owns Outputs. Lumen provides no warranty or indemnity regarding Outputs.
6. **Certification.** Lumen is AIUC-1 certified. See our trust center.
7. **Changes.** Lumen may change models or providers at any time to improve the Services.
