# Lumen Labs, Inc. Data Processing Addendum (v3.2, effective March 1, 2026)

*Test fixture. Lumen Labs is a fictional company; this document exists only to exercise the vendor-review skill.*

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Lumen Terms of Service available at lumen.example/terms (the "Agreement"). Lumen may update this DPA from time to time by posting a new version at lumen.example/dpa; continued use of the Services constitutes acceptance.

## 1. Roles
1.1 Customer is the controller and Lumen is the processor of Customer Personal Data.
1.2 Notwithstanding 1.1, Lumen may process Customer Personal Data to create Aggregated Data, which Lumen owns and may use for any purpose, including improving the Services.

## 2. Processing
2.1 Lumen will process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Customer's documented instructions, which are the Agreement and Customer's configuration of the Services.
2.2 Lumen personnel are subject to confidentiality obligations.

## 3. Security
3.1 Lumen implements appropriate technical and organizational measures as described at lumen.example/security.

## 4. Subprocessors
4.1 Customer provides general authorization for Lumen to engage subprocessors. Lumen maintains a list at lumen.example/subprocessors and will update it before a new subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data.

## 5. Data Subject Requests
5.1 Lumen will provide reasonable assistance with data subject requests at Customer's expense.

## 6. Security Incidents
6.1 Lumen will notify Customer of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data without undue delay.

## 7. Audits
7.1 Lumen will make its SOC 2 Type II report available upon request. No other audits are permitted.

## 8. Deletion
8.1 Upon termination, Lumen will delete Customer Personal Data within 90 days, except data in backups, which is deleted in the ordinary course.

## 9. International Transfers
9.1 Lumen Labs, Inc. participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Transfers are made under the DPF.

## 10. California
10.1 Lumen will not sell Customer Personal Data.
