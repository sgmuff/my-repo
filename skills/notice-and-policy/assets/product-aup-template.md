## What you may not do

When you use <product>, including its AI assistant, you may not:

- Try to get around its safeguards, including jailbreak attempts and prompt injection
- Try to get information you aren't entitled to, including other customers' data or the system's internal instructions
- Use it to create harmful, unlawful, harassing, or deceptive content
- Misuse other people's personal data
- Scrape or probe it by automated means, except through our published API terms
- Use it for any unlawful purpose

## What happens if you do

We monitor for misuse. When a request breaks this policy, we may block it and tell you why. Repeated or serious misuse may lead to suspension or termination of access under <terms>.
