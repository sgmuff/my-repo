# Brightpath Learning Privacy Notice

Last updated: March 3, 2025

Brightpath Learning, Inc. ("Brightpath," "we") provides classroom software to schools and a homework help app for families. This notice explains how we handle personal information.

## Information we collect

- Account information: name, email, school, and role.
- Usage information: pages viewed, features used, device type, and IP address.
- Support information: emails and chat messages you send our support team.

## How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our services, respond to support requests, and keep our services secure. We do not use student information for advertising.

## Sharing

We share information with service providers who help us run our services, such as hosting and email providers. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention

We keep personal information for 12 months after your account closes, then delete it.

## Your rights

You can ask to access or delete your information by emailing privacy@brightpath.example. We respond within 45 days.

## Children

Our homework help app is for users 13 and older. Schools provide consent for students under 13 who use our classroom software.
