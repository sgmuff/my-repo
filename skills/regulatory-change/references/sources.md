# Primary sources

Check these first. Secondary sources (law firm alerts, trackers, news) are good for finding a change, but confirm it against the primary source before changing a reference file. Record which you used and when.

| Regime | Primary sources | Useful secondary trackers |
|---|---|---|
| EU GDPR, Digital Omnibus | EUR-Lex (eur-lex.europa.eu); European Parliament Legislative Observatory (procedure 2025/0360(COD)); EDPB guidelines and opinions (edpb.europa.eu) | IAPP, law firm alerts |
| UK GDPR, DUAA | legislation.gov.uk (commencement regulations); ICO / Information Commission guidance | Stephenson Harwood DUAA tracker |
| Swiss FADP | fedlex.admin.ch; FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch) | — |
| EU AI Act | EUR-Lex consolidated text; Commission AI Office and AI Act Service Desk; Commission guidelines and codes of practice | artificialintelligenceact.eu |
| US state privacy laws | Each legislature's bill and statute site; attorney general and CPPA rulemaking pages (cppa.ca.gov); Colorado AG rules | IAPP US State Privacy Legislation Tracker |
| US breach laws | State statute sites; AG breach pages and forms | Law firm state breach matrices |
| FERPA, COPPA | eCFR; Federal Register; studentprivacy.ed.gov; ftc.gov COPPA rule and FAQ | — |
| State student privacy | State legislature and education department sites (for example, NYSED Part 121) | Student Data Privacy Consortium |
| NIST Privacy Framework, AI RMF | nist.gov/privacy-framework; nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework; CSRC publications | — |
| ISO/IEC 42001 and related | iso.org (catalog status only; the text is licensed) | — |
| AIUC-1 | standard.aiuc-1.com: changelog, llms.txt index, requirement pages, crosswalks, certification and scoping pages | — |

## Release and review calendar

These are review actions for the schedule, not calendar events:

- **AIUC-1:** January 15, April 15, July 15, October 15. Review within two weeks of each.
- **US state legislatures:** most sessions end by June; laws often take effect January 1 or July 1. Sweep in July and December.
- **EU:** check the Digital Omnibus procedure and AI Act implementing acts quarterly.
- **Reference file review dates:** `_shared/references/README.md`.
