# CLIENT ALERT: EU Adopts Digital Omnibus, Overhauling GDPR

*Hartwell & Pine LLP, October 2026*

The European Union has adopted the Digital Omnibus, the most significant change to the GDPR since it took effect in 2018. Following the European Parliament's plenary vote last week, companies should begin preparing now.

**Key changes**

1. **Definition of personal data.** Information is not personal data for an entity that cannot reasonably identify the individual, even if another entity could.
2. **Legitimate interest for AI.** A new provision confirms that developing and operating AI systems can rely on legitimate interests, subject to safeguards.
3. **Breach notification.** Notification to supervisory authorities is required only for breaches likely to result in a high risk, and the deadline moves from 72 to 96 hours, through a single EU reporting point.
4. **DPIA lists.** The EDPB will publish a single EU-wide list of processing that requires a DPIA.
5. **Cookies.** Consent rules for cookies move from the ePrivacy Directive into the GDPR, with new exemptions.

**What happens next.** The Parliament's position now goes to trilogue negotiations with the Council. The final text may differ. We expect agreement in 2027.

**What to do now.** Update your breach response plans to the 96-hour deadline and review your DPIA screening.
