## 1. Status

Member states had to transpose NIS2 by October 17, 2024 and apply it from October 18, 2024. Most transposed late; trackers in October 2026 report that France, Ireland, and Spain still had no national law in force, and the Commission has brought infringement proceedings, including a referral of Spain to the Court of Justice in July 2026. Trackers disagree on details, so check each state's official gazette. *Volatile:* the duties bind the company through national law, so check the transposing law in each member state where it is established or provides services, and its registration deadlines.

## 2. Whether the company is in scope

NIS2 applies to medium and large entities in the sectors of Annexes I and II, and to some entities regardless of size. Digital sectors relevant to a software company include cloud computing services, data center services, managed service providers, managed security service providers, online marketplaces, online search engines, and social networking platforms. Entities are **essential** or **important**, which changes supervision and fines, not the core duties.

Record: the sector and service, the size test, the member state of main establishment, and the national classification. A SaaS company is often outside NIS2 unless it is a cloud or managed service provider, or a supplier the regulated customers pass duties to by contract. *Verify* with counsel.

## 3. Cybersecurity risk-management measures (Art. 21)

Measures must be appropriate and proportionate to the risks, on an all-hazards basis, and include at least:

1. Risk analysis and information system security policies
2. Incident handling
3. Business continuity, backup management, disaster recovery, and crisis management
4. Supply chain security, including suppliers and service providers
5. Security in acquisition, development, and maintenance, including vulnerability handling and disclosure
6. Policies to assess the effectiveness of the measures
7. Basic cyber hygiene and cybersecurity training
8. Cryptography and, where appropriate, encryption
9. Human resources security, access control, and asset management
10. Multi-factor or continuous authentication, and secured communications where appropriate

For digital infrastructure and digital providers, Implementing Regulation 2024/2690 sets the technical and methodological detail.

## 4. Incident reporting (Art. 23)

For a significant incident: an early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware, an incident notification within 72 hours, and a final report within one month. Route incidents to `incident-assessment`, which also runs the GDPR clocks.

## 5. Management accountability (Art. 20)

Management bodies approve the risk-management measures, oversee their implementation, can be held liable, and must take training. Record the approver in the assessment.
