# ISO/IEC 42001 checklist

The text in §3 is a paraphrase, not ISO's wording. Don't quote it as the standard. In an audit or certification context, check each point against a licensed copy.

## Contents

1. How the standard is built
2. Using this checklist

---

## 1. How the standard is built

ISO/IEC 42001 is a management system standard on the same high-level structure as ISO/IEC 27001. Clauses 4 to 10 are mandatory for certification. Annex A lists 38 reference controls under 9 control objectives. An organization selects the controls it needs through its AI risk treatment and records each one, with any exclusions and reasons, in a **Statement of Applicability** (cl. 6.1.3). Annex B gives implementation guidance, Annex C lists AI-related objectives and risk sources, and Annex D covers use across domains and sectors.

Certification is by an accredited certification body, typically in a two-stage audit followed by surveillance audits.

## 2. Using this checklist

- **Gap assessments** (`program-assessment`, Wave 4): rate each row Met, Partial, Not met, or Not applicable, with evidence.
- **AI impact assessments** (`ai-impact-assessment`, Wave 2): cl. 6.1.4, cl. 8.4, and A.5.2–A.5.5 define what an AI system impact assessment covers.
- **AIUC-1 overlap:** the AIUC-1 column shows which AIUC-1 requirements support each row, with retired AIUC-1 requirements reassigned to their successors (`aiuc-1.md`, §6). The "AIUC coverage" column is AIUC's own rating of how well AIUC-1 covers the ISO requirement. Where AIUC rates a row as a Partial or Full gap, AIUC-1 evidence alone won't satisfy it.

One quirk of the source: AIUC's row for cl. 9.2.2 (internal audit programme) paraphrases management review. Treat 9.2.2 as requiring a planned internal audit programme, and 9.3 as covering management review.
