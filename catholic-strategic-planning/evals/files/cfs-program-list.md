# Catholic Family Services: Program List (for board retreat)

**Refugee resettlement.** Resettles about 180 refugees a year under a federal cooperative agreement, plus case management for 12 months after arrival. Funded almost entirely by government contracts. Staff of 14. Federal arrivals are expected to fall next year; the national office has warned of possible further cuts.

**Counseling.** Outpatient mental health counseling, sliding-scale fees, state Medicaid contract. 620 clients last year. Waitlist of six weeks. Two of eight therapists left this year; salaries are about 20% below the local market.

**Food pantry.** Serves about 1,100 households a month, up 35% in two years. Run by one staff coordinator and about 90 volunteers. Food mostly donated through the regional food bank; costs are rent share, a truck, and the coordinator.

**Pregnancy and parenting support.** Material help, mentoring, and parenting classes for about 140 mothers a year. Strong parish volunteer base. Donors give generously to this program specifically.

**Adoption services.** Domestic infant adoption. Placements fell from 14 (FY2022) to 4 (FY2026). Two staff. Fee revenue covers less than a third of cost.

**Senior home visiting.** Volunteer visitors for isolated seniors, coordinated by a part-time staff member. 45 seniors visited. Started by a parish group in 2019 and absorbed by the agency in 2021.

Notes from the director: board has not seen a program-by-program cost breakdown. We have not surveyed clients since 2019. The bishop has asked all diocesan agencies to "align with the diocesan pastoral plan," which is due out in spring.
